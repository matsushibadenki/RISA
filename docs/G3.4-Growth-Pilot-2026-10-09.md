The executable marginal-growth signal compares the first nonempty growth interval (48→96) with the last (192→384), avoiding fixed-header amortization. Marginal storage increases about 2.6%, rather than falling 10%. Even interpreting the protocol's first interval as empty→48 would not pass the joint signal because active structures never grow. Average bytes/Event falling about 10.7% is not evidence of learned condensation; preserved provenance Events and their graph records continue growing. Candidate description-length delta is zero because there are no candidates; total graph description length is not estimated by this metric.

## Independent evaluation and limitations

At every checkpoint, every seed: development/final role-aware accuracy 60/60; no-target-role accuracy 20/60. False generalization on the 20 unsupported-role cases is zero for both. The no-role readout abstains on positives, so the gain is transfer coverage, not a reduction of false generalization. Each partition has 180 distinct cases across seeds; the same cases are repeated at four checkpoints (720 evaluations), not 720 independent examples.

All actors, targets and case IDs are disjoint from training and from the other partition. Labels are never passed to training, adoption or tuning. Roles and contexts remain known and are explicitly supplied; this is not role induction, unseen composition, natural-language understanding or deployment evidence. Both partitions share a synthetic world definition/generator, so generator-level independence has not been established.

Every evaluated prediction-effect list matches after saving/reloading; evaluation and planning leave the complete learned snapshot unchanged. The planner's six expansions are a separate fixed ungrounded goal probe, not target-role planning success. No candidate is evaluated/adopted using held-out labels. All three seed workers complete within their unchanged 45s budgets. CPU and RSS profiling are not claims from this quality pilot; G3.3 performance measurements remain separate.

The predeclared exploratory joint signal fails at every seed: prediction gain and false-generalization conditions pass, active growth and marginal-storage conditions fail. Do not tune this fixture after inspecting final labels. This pilot is closed as an informative negative result for the joint growth hypothesis, not completion of G3.4 or grounds for a new runtime architecture.
