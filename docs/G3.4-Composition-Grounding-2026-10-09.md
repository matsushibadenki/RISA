# G3.4 composition target grounding repair

## English

The structural-growth pilot exposed eight false accepts per 24 composition cases: an unsupported `stored_in` target or reversed `powered_by` relation could reach `online`. Both candidate modes failed identically. Ordinary Primitive selection checked action, context, states and resources, but never checked the supplied target's role against its supporting Events.

The composer and one-step forecast now ground ordinary Primitive reuse against the union of target roles in primary supporting Event evidence whenever a target or target roles are supplied. A mismatch excludes that Primitive from every search step, so candidate-off composition cannot bypass role applicability. Explicit/induced query roles use the existing deterministic extractor. Abstract composition without a target and untyped legacy Primitive applicability retain their prior behavior. This repair does not validate relational plans beyond their existing candidate constraints, or claim deeper role refinement coverage.

The unchanged G3.4b manifest was rerun as a regression: three seeds, three checkpoints (24/48/96 Events), development/final panels of 24 cases each, and candidate-on/off. Every panel now scores **24/24**, with **zero false accepts** in both modes; full prediction/composition reload differences remain zero. Valid powered/cooled targets, state prerequisites and numeric energy boundaries are preserved. A focused test also covers one-step forecast and target-free abstract composition. This uses previously inspected development/final panels and is a repair regression, not a fresh independent final evaluation.

An existing temporal-candidate regression (seed23,16 development/40 final episodes) now has 100% ordinary-path success, matching the candidate: the unchanged adoption gate correctly rejects its redundant gain (previously25 percentage points). The actor/target relational regression (seed29,20/40 episodes) retains candidate100% versus ordinary60%, a40-point gain instead of60. Its ordinary path still has false-generalization rate0.5, so role grounding alone does not enforce all relational constraints. Historical candidate gains must be read in light of this stronger baseline.

Candidate counts remain ten, active Primitives four and adopted candidates zero. The growth/condensation hypothesis remains unproven: this is an applicability correction, not candidate benefit. Next is an independent, preregistered quality comparison with shared role grounding and strong grounded-table controls; additional scale or candidate adoption should not hide the current null growth result.

## 日本語

同じmanifestの3 seed・3 checkpoint・development/final各24件を再実行し、候補on/off両方で24/24正解、誤受理0となった。保存復元時の完全予測・合成の差も0。正常な構造対象、前提状態、energy境界、対象なし合成を回帰テストで確認した。既に確認済みのfinal panelを用いた修正回帰であり、新規独立finalではない。

過去の時間系列回帰（seed23）では既存経路が75%→100%となり、候補の追加効果は0、既存採用gateで冗長候補として拒否された。actor/target関係回帰（seed29）は候補100%・既存60%で40ポイントの利得が残るが、既存経路の誤一般化率0.5は未解決。

候補10、active Primitive4、採用候補0のままで、成長・凝縮仮説は未実証。次に共通の役割接地と強い具体遷移表を使う独立品質比較を事前固定する。

## 简体中文

G3.4b错误接受未支持`stored_in`及反向`powered_by`目标，原因是普通Primitive选择仅检查action、情境、状态及资源，没有检查目标角色。现在指定目标或角色时，合成及一步forecast会对支持Event中的目标角色进行匹配，在每个搜索step排除不匹配Primitive。无目标抽象合成及无角色证据的旧Primitive保持原有适用性。

使用相同manifest重新运行三个seed、三个checkpoint及开发/最终各24case，两种候选模式均24/24正确，误受理为零，完整预测/合成重载差异为零。回归测试覆盖正常结构目标、状态及energy边界、无目标合成。这是已检查最终面板的修复回归，不是新独立最终评估。

旧时间系列回归（seed23）普通路径75%→100%，候选增益为零，原采纳gate拒绝冗余候选。actor/target关系回归（seed29）候选100%、普通60%，仍有40点增益，但普通路径误泛化率0.5尚未解决。

候选仍十个、活跃Primitive四个、采纳候选零，增长/凝聚假说仍未证明。下一步预先固定共享角色接地及强具体转移表的独立质量比较。
