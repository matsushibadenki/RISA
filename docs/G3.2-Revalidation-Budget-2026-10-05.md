# G3.2 online revalidation lifetime and label cost

## English

### Fixed development design

Five seeds × seven arms × three policies × two label caps produce **210 runs**. Policies are expiry-only, unchanged-parent reactivation only, and reactivation with independent revalidation after A2 Events 4, 8 and 12. Caps are 1,200 and 4,800 labels per run, available equally to every arm and the baseline controls. B-boundary adoption uses the previous fixed A-role indoor/sheltered/warm-union contracts. Missing proposals consume no labels; already adopted candidates are skipped. Each fresh revalidation cycle has unique development/final probe, episode and source namespaces, 200 probes per partition and unchanged bootstrap/gain gates.

Revalidation here means **full independent development/final re-adoption of current proposals**, not automatic validation inheritance or the cheaper `CandidateExtensionValidator` path. Changed evidence receives fresh verification. The schedule and maximum reservation (1,200 labels per opportunity) are fixed before scoring. Insufficient reservation blocks the entire opportunity before mutation, including when some candidate contracts would cost less. This conservative allocation is part of the control, not an optimal budget allocator. It is not an independent final evaluation.

The runner now forwards its existing read-only pre-update diagnostic hook through `run_aba`. Twelve A nuisance panels are scored **before** the next A2 Event is learned, giving 24 × 12 × 5 = 1,440 probe exposures per arm. These repeat a fixed diagnostic panel to measure prediction lifetime; they are not 1,440 independent held-out episodes and do not enter validation or learning. Correctness immediately after each scheduled validation and at exit is recorded separately. Final-boundary revalidation cannot improve predictions earlier in the phase.

### Results across five seeds

| Policy / cap | Arm | Actual labels | Correct A2 pre-update exposures /1,440 | A2 exit A correct /120 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| expiry-only / either cap | full | 6,000 | 96 | 0 |
| reactivation-only / either cap | full | 6,000 | 168 | 0 |
| scheduled / 1,200 | full | 6,000 | 168 | 0 |
| scheduled / 4,800 | full | 22,000 | **552** | 80 |
| scheduled / 4,800 | no-merge | **14,000** | **552** | 80 |
| scheduled / 4,800 | no-dormancy | 22,000 | 552 | 80 |
| latest/count two-hop subset tables | baseline | **0** | **1,440** | **120** |

With the 1,200 cap, all 15 scheduled full-arm opportunities are blocked after initial B adoption. Increasing available budget alone has no effect on expiry/reactivation-only policies. With the 4,800 cap, full-arm actual per-seed consumption is 4,800 / 4,000 / 4,400 / 4,400 / 4,400: valid unchanged candidates need no repeat labels. No-merge consumes 3,200 / 2,400 / 2,800 / 2,800 / 2,800. All label IDs are unique across B/A2 and consumption stays within the cap in all 210 runs.

Full-arm pre-update correct counts by A2 Event, summed over five seeds, are **80, 48, 32, 8, 80, 48, 40, 24, 80, 56, 32, 24**. No-merge has the identical sequence. Refreshes restore the warm scope, but its benefit decays as new evidence invalidates adoption. B nuisance correctness remains zero: only A warm contracts were validated. The 80/120 exit accuracy follows validation after the last A2 Event; persistence through another learning update is not demonstrated by that exit number.

Relative to reactivation-only, full gains 384 correct repeated probe exposures using 16,000 additional labels, while no-merge gains the same 384 using 10,000 additional labels. These are fixture-specific measurement costs, not sample-complexity claims. Strong tables need no adoption labels and retain all A/B nuisance answers. Native exact-context readout is already strong, so these results do not demonstrate a recovery or planner advantage. A1 nuisance accuracy is zero; new A-scope knowledge obtained during B cannot be described as retention of previously working A1 nuisance predictions.

### Verification and limits

The result retains label IDs, complete adoption decisions, budget-block traces, pre-update lifetime traces, ordinary drift/replay/touch metrics and reload checks. PredictionResults agree after JSON reload at 1,260 lifecycle checkpoints. Tests cover fresh labels, unique cycles, budget blocking, invalid schedules and the difference between final-boundary correctness and earlier prediction lifetime. Baseline lifetime traces use the same shared deterministic two-hop role projection and observed Events.

The cue is still a direct relational regime separator, Primitive split opportunities remain unsatisfied, and the fixed warm contracts require external applicability supervision. This development control therefore cannot establish G3.2 causal superiority or satisfy its independent-final gate. Both low- and high-budget outcomes are preserved; no threshold was relaxed to manufacture adoption.

### Next

Keep these controls as a cost/expiry regression. First audit whether additions outside an adopted candidate's scope should invalidate its applicability verification: the candidate's scoped evidence and its broader parent's evidence have different roles. Compare a budget-accounted query-backed extension check with full re-adoption, preserving explicit in-scope counterexample rejection, role/lineage constraints and strong subset baselines. Only an informative contrast justifies independent final evaluation or scaling this protocol.

## 日本語

5 seed・7条件・3方針・2予算の210実行で、検証失効のみ、親再活性化のみ、再活性化＋A2の4/8/12 Event後の独立再検証を比較した。全条件に1,200または4,800ラベル上限を与える。再検証は各回200 development＋200 finalの新しいquery付き適用ラベルで現在の候補を再採用する方式であり、検証の無条件継承や軽量extension validatorではない。採用が有効な候補は再検証せず、予算予約不足なら変更前に停止する。

A2の各Eventを学習する前に未見文脈24 probeを採点し、5 seed×12更新の1,440件を予測有効期間の指標とした。同じ診断probeの反復であり、1,440件の独立episodeではない。再検証直後や終了時の正解数とは分けて記録した。

全機構ありの更新前正解は、失効のみ96/1,440、再活性化のみ168/1,440、4,800上限での再検証552/1,440。再検証の実消費は5 seed合計22,000ラベル。一方、mergeなしも同じ552/1,440に達し、消費は14,000ラベルで済む。両方のA2終了正解は80/120だが、最後のEvent後にも再検証しているため、その後の学習更新に耐えた証拠ではない。1,200上限では全機構ありの再検証機会15回すべてが予算不足で停止した。

強い2-hop文脈部分集合表は採用ラベル0で更新前1,440/1,440、終了時A/B各120/120を維持した。RISAの優位性は未実証。A1未見文脈精度が0なので、Bで獲得したA予測をA1知識の保持とは呼ばない。210実行すべてでラベルIDの一意性と上限を確認し、1,260 checkpointで保存前後の完全PredictionResult一致を確認した。

次に、候補自身の範囲内証拠と広い親の範囲外証拠を区別して検証失効を監査する。明示的な範囲内反例・役割・lineage制約を維持し、予算を計上したquery付きextension再検証と完全再採用を比較する。独立finalとPrimitive splitのゲートは未達。
