# G3.2 Context-Conditioned Role Discovery

## English

The relational return fixture revealed a second gap after readout alignment:
strict candidate discovery produces zero candidates. Its global role-collision
rule requires an induced role's outcomes to be constant, including across
indoor/sheltered/outdoor contexts. Even a two-hop role therefore fails when the
same structure has legitimate context-dependent outcomes.

An opt-in state policy, `context_conditioned_role_refinement=True`, now detects
one-hop collisions within each exact normalized context. If two-hop roles
resolve those within-context collisions, discovery can retain them even though
outcomes differ across contexts. Candidate grouping still uses role/outcome
and keeps the other outcomes as explicit counterexamples. Context
specialization and sibling merge must still satisfy their existing diversity
and precision requirements. Adoption does not happen automatically. Global
collision checking remains the default; unresolved same-role, same-context
collisions still produce no candidates in the opt-in mode. Role refinement
counts retain the existing per-base budget.

The fixed manifest pairs both policies over five seeds with native two-hop
readouts, identical Events, Replay schedule and probes. The measured drift
trajectory has zero available/consumed adoption labels in both policies.

| Boundary | Default candidates | Opt-in base / specialized / merged |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A1, 6 Events | 0 | 2 / 3 / 1 |
| B, 18 cumulative Events | 0 | 4 / 6 / 2 |
| A2, 30 cumulative Events | 0 | 4 / 6 / 2 |

The opt-in candidates have depth one at A1 and depth two after B reveals a
within-context collision. Summed base counterexample counts are 6, 18 and 30
at the respective boundaries. All candidates in the measured trajectory remain
proposed. Both policies have B and immediate A-return accuracy 1.0 because the
aligned readout already solves the fixture. Candidate growth alone adds no
prediction benefit. Full candidates and complete predictions match reload at
all 30 checkpoints (two policies × five seeds × three boundaries).

## Query-backed adoption diagnostic

Applicability and extension probes can now carry an optional complete
`PredictionQuery`. For single-transition candidates, the evaluator checks
action, inferred/supplied target role and context, using the same candidate-role
matching as prediction. Parent comparisons use the same check. Query and probe
contexts must agree; unsupported query-backed sequence candidates are rejected.
Legacy probes without a query remain context-only and cannot validate a new
role boundary.

At B, a disposable copy receives an independent development panel of 200 labels
and an independent final panel of 200 labels. The fixed A-scope warm oracle is
applicable to A indoor/sheltered, and not to B or outdoor/exposed queries. The
panel varies novel identities and includes both observed edge directions. The
label cap is 400 for both policies; default discovery has no candidate and
consumes zero, while opt-in consumes 400 in every seed. The A warm merge passes
with candidate accuracy 1.0, baseline accuracy 0.75 and gain 0.125 over the
strongest individual parent. Both independent partitions pass existing gates.
Parents remain proposed; this diagnostic does not exercise ancestor dormancy.

After copy-only adoption, 16 of 48 nuisance-tagged A/B predictions per seed
change from abstention to a correct warm outcome: 80/240 across five seeds.
Only one A warm merge is adopted, so this is partial transfer, not complete
A/B accuracy. Adopted predictions remain identical after reload. Removing the
queries from otherwise identical development labels makes the context-only
comparison reject the merge, confirming that role boundaries matter here.
The adoption copy never affects subsequent A2 observations or the measured
drift trajectory. The 400 labels are explicitly counted as a separate
post-B diagnostic and are not a recovery or pre-return retention gain. The
existing strong subset/two-hop tables remain required; superiority over them
has not been demonstrated.

Schema v6 persists the discovery policy alongside the readout depth. States
through v5 without the field use the strict policy. Earlier runtimes reject v6
instead of reconstructing adopted candidates under a different discovery rule.
Configure policies before learning; changing them directly on a learned state
is unsupported. No independent final G3.2 run has been performed.

Next: freeze the initial candidate pool, role-aware validation panels, available
label cap, online adoption schedule and mechanism execution conditions across
all seven arms. Reuse these controls when checking candidate survival after
consistent extensions and rejection after true in-scope contradiction. Proposal
availability and the copy-only adoption pilot are not completion of G3.2.

    target_role_readout_hops=2,
    context_conditioned_role_refinement=True,

## 简体中文

原有role冲突规则要求所有情境的结果一致，因此会排除合法的情境相关候选。可选`context_conditioned_role_refinement=True`以两跳role解决同一情境内的冲突，仍保留其他结果作为反例，并维持现有特化、合并和采纳门槛。五个seed中候选在A1为6、B/A2为12；同role同情境仍无法解决的冲突继续排除。全部候选尚未采纳，漂移预测及保留准确率仍与原控制相同为100%，不能把候选数量增加当作智能改善。

新增query支持的适用探针，在候选和父候选比较中同时验证action、role、context。仅在B状态副本上使用独立development/final各200标签后，五个seed均采纳A-scope warm merge；240个nuisance探针中80个从弃权变为正确，但仅代表部分转移。原A2学习轨迹不变，不能报告为恢复或保留收益。两组可用上限均为400，实际消费默认0、可选策略400。父候选仍未采纳，因此未证明休眠。30个checkpoint中完整候选及预测的重载均一致。

schema v6保存生成策略，旧state缺省采用原严格策略。下一步固定初始候选、role-aware标签、共用上限及在线采纳时间，开展七组条件的机制执行及独立验证。尚未证明优于强表基线。
