## Next gate / 次の門槛 / 下一门槛

[Next] Construct an online lifecycle schedule with **separate** candidate-development probes, candidate-adoption probes and experiment-final probes. Keep all three disjoint from training and candidate ancestry; report every supervised validation label as part of the adaptation budget. Require nonzero executed splits, adopted merges and dormancy transitions in the appropriate arms before interpreting outcome contrasts. If the ordinary lifecycle cannot satisfy this without manual status assignment, report that as a G3.2 design failure and revise the lifecycle before claiming a mechanism benefit.

[Next] 候補development、候補採用、実験finalのprobeを三重に分離し、祖先証拠とも重ねないオンライン生命周期を作る。採用判断で使った教師ラベル数も適応予算に含める。該当条件でsplit実行・merge採用・休眠遷移が非ゼロになるまで、成績差を因果効果と呼ばない。
