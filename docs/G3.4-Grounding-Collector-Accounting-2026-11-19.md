English: Counter-only prefix/skip updates need only a counter-size bound; sample updates overestimate new joint payload and list growth. No per-Primitive accounting ledger is retained: only one integer per window. The existing reachable-stream budget remains distinct from RSS, temporary peaks and exports. Exhaustion still freezes collection and retains prefix bias.

简体中文: prefix/skip不改样本的位置仅估计计数器大小，其余更新高估新联合payload和list增长。无逐Primitive台账，仅每窗口增加一个整数。预算仍是可达流对象而非RSS、临时峰值或导出副本；耗尽后冻结及前缀偏差不变。

## Comparison / 比較 / 比较

The reference subclass bypasses the new bound calculation and executes the original full-accounting path. Compare equal 32,768-byte and 4,096-byte budgets on independent states and identical queries. Require equal sample digests/TV, counts/stopping, complete results and persistence; measured calibration may still change lookup allocation.

对照subclass跳过新上界计算，执行原全量计量。双方使用相同32,768及4,096字节预算、独立state和相同query，比较样本digest/TV、计数/停止、完整结果及保存；基于实测校准的lookup分配可不同。

日本語: 32,768-byte予算の少数長列ではCPU中央値比88.2%減。新しいkeyが多く早期停止する条件は改善せず約1%悪化した。保持量・標本・停止位置は同じであり、coverageを犠牲にした速度改善ではない。無制限collectorとの優位やRSS削減は主張しない。

English: At 32,768 bytes, few long streams reduce the ratio of median CPU times by 88.2%. Many-new-key/early-freeze cases are about 1% slower. Samples, stopping and retained bytes match, so the gain does not trade away coverage. This is not a claim of superiority to uncapped collection or lower RSS.

简体中文: 32,768字节预算下少数长流的CPU中位数比减少88.2%；新key多且早停时约慢1%。样本、停止和保留量一致，非牺牲覆盖换取提速；不宣称优于无限额采样或降低RSS。
